Product Overview

The BamBed for Chevy Colorado and GMC Canyon is a three-panel sleeping platform engineered for convenience and strength. It’s designed to sit above the wheel wells, offering a full 60” wide sleeping area. The modular panels are removable in under two minutes with no tools required, making setup and storage simple. When not in use, the panels are compact enough to fit in a closet or garage.

Made from high-quality birch plywood, the platform supports up to 1,500 lbs of evenly distributed weight and comes with a lifetime Strength Guarantee for the original owner. Whether you’re heading off-road or camping in your local area, this DIY truck bed camper provides peace of mind and reliability.

How to Install and Use

Installing the BamBed platform is straightforward:

  1. Slide the three panels into position above the wheel wells.
  2. Use the included hardware to secure the panels (no tools required).
  3. Optional tailgate extension snaps into place to increase length.
  4. Add optional sliders or tie-downs for organized cargo storage and secure off-road travel.

When not in use, panels can be removed and stored compactly in a garage or closet. This tool-free, fast setup ensures your truck bed remains versatile for both work and play.
